CoupleCup Privacy Policy

Privacy notice under KVKK · Version 1 · Effective date: October 24, 2026 · Türkçe

This is an English translation of the Turkish original. If the two versions differ, the Turkish version prevails.

In short:

CoupleCup is an app where two people share memories, letters, plans, a question of the day and more in a shared space.

In the app, only you and your partner see what you write. Data is encrypted in transit and on the server.

We don’t show ads, sell your data or use ad networks. Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports that help us improve the app are collected only if you allow it.

You can download your data at any time and delete your account from inside the app.

1. Data controller

CoupleCup is developed and published under the name Two Fox Labs by Yunus Emre Suna (Gaziantep, Türkiye). Yunus Emre Suna is the data controller under Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (“KVKK”).

Contact: twofoxlabs@gmail.com

2. What this policy covers

This policy explains the personal data processed when you use the CoupleCup mobile app (Android and iOS). It also includes the information required under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users in the European Union.

3. What data we process

Account information

  • Your email address and password. Your password is never stored in plain text; only an irreversible hash is kept.
  • One-time codes sent to your email to verify your account and reset your password.
  • If you sign in with Google: the email address and Google account ID that Google sends us. Google also sends your name and a link to your profile photo; these stay in the sign-in record, aren’t used in the app and aren’t shown to your partner. We never see your Google password. In this case no separate password is kept in the app.
  • If you sign in with Apple (iPhone): the email address Apple sends us and an account ID tied to your Apple Account. If you choose to hide your email, Apple gives us a private address that forwards to you. On your first sign-in Apple also sends your name; it is only suggested in the name field of your profile, and you can change it. We never see your Apple Account password. When you delete your account, CoupleCup’s link to your Apple Account is removed too.
  • During sign-up, sign-in, code requests and password resets, a security check (Cloudflare Turnstile) runs to prevent automated attacks. During this check your IP address and technical information about your device or browser are sent to Cloudflare. We are only told whether the check passed.

Profile information

  • Your name, avatar and gender (Female / Male). Your avatar is either your initial with a color you choose, or a cartoon character you design in the app (choices such as hair, eyes and accessories).
  • Your date of birth, if you add it. If you do, a “Birthday” special day is created automatically in your shared space.
  • Your profile is visible to your partner. No photos are uploaded.

Pairing information

  • The date you paired with your partner and, if you add it, the date your relationship began.
  • Invite codes. The code itself isn’t stored on the server; only a hash is kept for verification. A code is valid for 24 hours and can be used once.
  • Wrong code attempts, to prevent abuse. These are deleted after 7 days.

Content in your shared space

Everything you and your partner write in the app is stored in your shared space:

  • answers to the question of the day;
  • memories and memory notes;
  • letters;
  • special days;
  • plan lists;
  • dates you plan together;
  • wheel options and results;
  • likes and votes;
  • timer records and goals.

For each item we also keep who wrote it and when.

Location

When you add a place to a date or a memory, a map opens. If you haven’t picked a place before, the app asks for location permission. If you allow it, the map opens where you are. Your current location is used on your phone and isn’t saved.

If you pick a point on the map, only that point’s coordinates are saved with the date or memory. When you search for a place, the text you type and the area the map is showing at that moment are sent to Google through our server to find results. If the map opened at your location, that area is your approximate location.

If you don’t allow it, the map still works. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your phone’s settings.

Notification information

  • Your device’s notification token (push token), if you want notifications.
  • Your notification preferences and quiet hours.
  • Your in-app notification inbox.

Settings and consents

  • Your theme and language choices and your home screen layout.
  • Consents you give and withdraw. The date of each consent and the version of the text you agreed to are recorded.

Kept only on your device

  • Your session information.
  • Theme and language cache and small screen markers such as “seen”.
  • Whether the app lock is on or off. The app lock uses your phone’s fingerprint, face recognition or screen lock. Your biometric data never leaves your phone; we can’t see it.
  • The file created when you download your data. It is prepared on your phone and is never uploaded to the server or sent by email.

Usage statistics and crash reports (only with your consent)

To improve the app, anonymous usage statistics and crash reports may be collected. Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are used for this. This collection:

  • Happens only if you explicitly consent. Consent is off by default.
  • Includes only event names, such as which screen was opened, and a technical error log when the app crashes.
  • Never includes your memories, letters, answers, name, email or any information about your partner.
  • Can be stopped at any time by withdrawing your consent in Settings. Collection stops as soon as you do.

What we don’t collect

We don’t collect photos, your contact list, microphone audio, your advertising ID or health data. We don’t track or store your location.

5. Who can see your data

  • Your partner. In the app, only you and your partner see the content in your shared space and your profile.
  • Question of the day: your partner’s answer isn’t shown to you until you write your own answer or the day ends.
  • Sealed letters: the title and text are never sent to the recipient’s device before the opening time. These rules are enforced on the server, not in the app’s interface.
  • No end-to-end encryption. This means it isn’t a system where “only you can see” your data. Your data travels over an encrypted connection and is stored on encrypted disks on the server. When technical maintenance or security requires it, the system administrator can technically access the data. This access isn’t used in day-to-day work.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app under Settings → Delete account. If you can’t access the app, just write to twofoxlabs@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account.

  • If you aren’t paired with a partner: your account and all your information are deleted immediately.
  • If you are paired with a partner: deleting your account also ends the pairing. The following are deleted immediately:
    • your email address, password, Google or Apple sign-in record and sessions;
    • your push token, notifications and settings;
    • your consent records;
    • your date of birth.
    The content in your shared space is kept closed for 6 months so that your partner can download it, and is then permanently deleted. During this period we keep only an irreversible hash of your email address. It is used so that your shared space can come back if you sign up again with the same email and pair with the same person. When the period ends, it is deleted too.

9. Your rights

Under Article 11 of KVKK and the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Learn whether your personal data is processed and request information about it.
  • Learn the purpose of the processing and whether your data is used in line with that purpose.
  • Know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom your data is transferred.
  • Ask for your data to be corrected if it is incomplete or inaccurate. You can edit your profile yourself in the app.
  • Ask for your data to be deleted or destroyed. You can delete your account yourself in the app.
  • Ask for corrections and deletions to be notified to the third parties to whom your data was transferred.
  • Object to an outcome against you that results from analysis by automated systems.
  • Claim compensation if you suffer damage because your data was processed unlawfully.
  • Get a copy of your data. With Settings → Download your data, you can get your memories, letters, answers, special days, plans and timer records as a PDF or text file.
  • Withdraw your explicit consent at any time, in Settings → Permissions.

Send your requests to twofoxlabs@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account. We handle requests free of charge within 30 days at the latest.

If you aren’t satisfied with the outcome, you can file a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority. If you live in the EU, you can contact the data protection authority in your country.

10. Security

  • Every connection between the app and the server is encrypted (TLS).
  • Data is stored on encrypted disks on the server.
  • Who can read which data is decided by the database’s own access rules: only the members of a shared space can read its content.
  • Passwords are stored in an irreversible form.
  • If you like, you can tie the app to your phone’s lock with the app lock.

No system is entirely risk-free. If a security breach occurs, we will inform the people affected and the Authority within the time limits set by law.

11. Age limit

CoupleCup is for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 13, don’t use the app.

If we learn that we are processing data belonging to someone under 13, we will delete that account and its data. If you notice such a case, please write to us.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. If there is a significant change, we will let you know in the app. If needed, we will ask for your consent again.
